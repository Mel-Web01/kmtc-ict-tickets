FAQs

Common issues and quick fixes before escalating a ticket

Printer not working?

Check it's powered on, connected to the network, and has paper/toner. Restart the printer if it's still unresponsive.

Can't log into the student/staff portal?

Confirm you're using the correct student/staff number, and try resetting your password via the portal's "Forgot Password" link.

No internet connection?

Restart your device, reconnect to Wi-Fi, and check if others in the same room are also affected (may be a wider network issue).

ABN Unisol (ERP) not loading?

Clear your browser cache, try a different browser, and confirm you're using the correct login URL.

How long does a ticket take to resolve?

High urgency tickets are prioritized within 24 hours, medium within 3 days, and low within 7 days.
